About Us

Our Mission: Bring physics to younger students, especially girls, everywhere.

We want to motivate kids to discover their interest in physics, no matter what level they are at. Kids are curious about how the world works, and with physics knowledge, they can satiate that curiosity. This passion could grow into a love of learning and solving problems for a lifetime. This competition is an opportunity for them to learn and then have friendly competition with other kids their age. It's also a chance for like-minded students to meet up. Do it for the prizes, the glory, or simply the love of the game!

Meet the Team

Founder/President
Yinuo Zhou

1th grade, Bergen County Academies NJ

Usually referred to as Vivian, this girl took a physics class in school one day and decided that it was quite fascinating. She wished that she had known such a fun subject existed earlier. After that, she joined a handful of physics clubs and realized there was a large gender disparity (which irked her more than just a little; everybody deserves to experience the joys of physics). She thought that it'd be fun if there was a physics competition geared towards bubbly younger kids and encouraged girls especially to explore the subject, whether or not they had officially begun learning physics. So, she started this organization. Since beginning to work on this project back in the summer of 2025, she was able to find some wonderful teammates to write physics problems with her!

Massachussets Regions Director
Ariel Fu

11th grade, Hopkinton High School MA

One day in her freshman year, Ariel took her first physics class at school. After many quizzes, tests, and real world examples, including the robotics team she cofounded in the same year, she discovered that she enjoyed using her hands to make changes in the world and learning about and experiencing physics in real life. She became interested in helping other younger children experience the joy she felt when learning physics, so she is very excited to be a part of organizing a physics competition for kids.
