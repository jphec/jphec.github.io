What is JPhEC?

The beginning to a long road of learning.

***Updated as of 10/08/2026 -- please read throroughly!
JPhEC is an opportunity for all kids grades *6-10 to let their abilities in physics shine. It is a competition featuring a 3 hour, 20 question exam of moderate difficulty, with open-ended questions. Before and after the main test there will be fun activities, such as presentations and speed quiz rounds for review(HOW DO YOU MEASURE THE VELOCITY OF FLYING PIGS?? ;D). It doesn't require extensive studying.
Our competition is geared towards those with a growing and inquisitive mind. Prior physics knowledge is great, but not necessary to participate!
Top scorers will earn monetary prizes (up to 150$!!). There will also be extra prizes for the top three girls!!
The test was be available strictly online on March 14th, 2027.
