FAQ

Do I need to know physics prior to this?
It is recommended to study from the textbook on the Resources page! Intuition and math would be great tools for the competition too.

How do I study?
Use the Resources page on the website! It has links to problem sets, textbooks, etc.

Is it online?
Yes, the competition is online this year on March 14th, 2027.

Are there sample problems I can study from?
The Resources page on the website has a google drive under "Sample Problems" with some sample problems! Please also take a look at the recommended courses.

What do I do with my partner?
Their score and your score add together to see if you qualify for the duo prize. THIS IS AN INDIVIDUAL COMPETITION. DO NOT BE IN THE SAME ROOM WITH THEM, DO NOT TALK TO THEM OR COMMUNICATE ABOUT THE COMPETITION IN ANY WAY UNTIL ANSWERS ARE SUBMITTED.
